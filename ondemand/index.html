Before you start.

Will I be charged anything today?
No. We save a card so we can bill meetings that are actually held, but nothing is charged now. You only ever pay your price per qualified meeting that is held, on the rules you set. If we never book one, you never pay.
What if the meetings are bad?
You don't pay for them. A meeting only bills when it matches the ICP and criteria you set, the prospect shows and stays, and it is confirmed objectively. No-shows, reschedules, and wrong-fit never bill.
How is deliverability handled?
We build the infrastructure first and match it to each prospect's email provider, run premium verification to keep bounce rates low, rotate infrastructure before it degrades, and manage blocklists so we never touch your active customers.
How fast do meetings start?
First meetings usually land in weeks 3 to 4 as infrastructure warms. You add a card now with no charge, and only pay per held meeting after.
What counts as a qualified meeting?
Title, company size, and geo match the ICP you set, the prospect attends for at least your chosen minimum minutes, and it is verified by your booking tool plus join data. You lock those rules in onboarding, so there is nothing to argue about later.
Can I stop?
Any time. There is no long-term contract. You are only ever charged for meetings that are held.
Who sends the emails, and is this spammy?
We do, from sending infrastructure we build and warm for you. Every email is written one to one from real research on that prospect, never a blast. That is exactly why they reach the primary inbox and get replies instead of getting flagged.
How do you confirm a meeting was actually held?
Against your booking link plus join data, on the rules you set: title, company size, geo, and a minimum time the prospect stayed. Anything that does not match never bills. You lock those rules in onboarding, so there is nothing to argue about later.
Will this work for my niche?
If you are US B2B software with a real sales team, almost always. We run a quick fit check right after you start, and if your market cannot be booked profitably, we tell you straight and you are not charged.
