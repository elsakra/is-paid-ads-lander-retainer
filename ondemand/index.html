Before you start.

What if the meetings are bad?
You do not pay for them. A meeting only bills when it matches the ICP and criteria you set, the prospect shows and stays past your minimum time, and it is confirmed by calendar plus join data. No-shows, reschedules, and wrong-fit never bill.
How is deliverability handled?
We build the sending infrastructure first and match it to each prospect's email provider, run premium verification to keep bounce rates low, rotate infrastructure before it degrades, and manage blocklists so we never touch your active customers. It is the part most agencies skip, and it is why our email reaches the primary inbox.
How fast do meetings start?
First meetings usually land in weeks 3 to 4 as infrastructure warms. Your deposit covers that setup, and you only ever pay per held meeting after that.
What exactly counts as a qualified meeting?
Exactly what you set in the config: title, company size, and geo match your ICP, the prospect attends for at least your chosen minimum minutes, and it is verified objectively. You lock those rules at checkout, so there is nothing to argue about later.
Can I stop?
Any time. There is no long-term contract. Pause or stop and whatever is left of your deposit is yours.
Why a deposit instead of paying per meeting after the fact?
The deposit covers setup and your first meetings, and we draw your bid from it as meetings are held. It keeps money up front and means there is never a charge after the fact to dispute. You top up when it runs low.
